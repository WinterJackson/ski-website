Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 Jurisdiction: Republic of Kenya & International Operator: Winter Jackson / Xinteck Digital Solutions
Privacy Policy Cookies & Storage Terms of Service

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Ski (the “Extension”) and its companion website at ski.xinteck.co.ke (the “Website”), developed and maintained by Winter Jackson in collaboration with Xinteck Digital Solutions (Nairobi, Republic of Kenya), handle information.

Ski is engineered on a strict Zero-Telemetry, Local-First architecture. Neither the Extension nor the Website collects, sells, rents, profiles, or transmits personal data to us or to third-party advertisers.

2. Regulatory Compliance (Kenya & International)

We align our privacy practices with:

  • Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (Cap. 411C, Laws of Kenya) and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, regulated by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).
  • Kenya Information and Communications Act (Cap. 411A) and the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018 (Kenya).
  • EU & UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (Articles 5, 6, and 12–22).
  • California Consumer Privacy Act / Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) — we do not sell or share personal information.
  • Browser Store Policies: Chrome Web Store User Data Policy (Limited Use), Mozilla Firefox Add-ons (AMO) Data Disclosure Policy, and Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store Policies.

4. Website Data & Third-Party Services

Our static website stores only your dark/light theme preference locally in your browser (ski-theme in localStorage). If you voluntarily click external links, those independent platforms operate under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub (source code, releases, and issue tracker) — governed by GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Mozilla Add-ons (AMO) / Edge / Chrome Web Store — governed by Mozilla, Microsoft, and Google store policies.
  • Paystack (optional voluntary financial support via paystack.shop) — payment details are processed solely by Paystack; we never see or store card or M-Pesa credentials.
  • Hosting & Fonts: Static hosting infrastructure (such as Vercel) may record standard ephemeral HTTP server logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamp) solely for DDoS mitigation and uptime security.

5. Your Rights Under Section 26 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 & GDPR

Under Section 26 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) and equivalent international laws, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of the use to which any personal data is put (Section 29);
  • Access personal data held about you;
  • Object to the processing of all or part of your personal data;
  • Request correction of false or misleading data, or deletion/erasure of irrelevant or unlawfully held data.

Because Ski collects zero personal data, you can exercise full erasure at any time simply by clearing your browser’s local storage or uninstalling the extension. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in Kenya (odpc.go.ke) or your local supervisory authority.

6. Children’s Privacy & Contact

Ski does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 years of age (consistent with Section 33 of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019). For privacy inquiries, open an issue on GitHub or reach us via Xinteck Digital Solutions.
